# Long Tua product

## Who

Whoever keeps the books of a Thai SME.
Solo founder, or founder plus an accountant.
The product does not model who does which task.
Any login may capture, confirm, reconcile, or file.
Access may differ by role (`owner`, `admin`, `manager`, `staff`, `accountant`, `viewer`) — those roles are suite-wide, not invented here.

They know their transactions better than Thai tax rules.
They capture evidence as it happens.
They reconcile in passes, not continuously.

## Job

Make the monthly close tractable: collect documents, confirm AI extraction, reconcile the bank, file what the shop owes.

## Done (non-VAT shop, suite v1 segment)

Income, expense, documents, bank reconciliation, WHT.
VAT modules (PP30, §87, ABB apparatus) stay gated until the tenant is VAT-registered.
That gated experience must feel complete, not crippled.

## Not this

- Filing ABB / PP30 for a non-VAT shop.
- POS computing filings.
- Staff becoming the statutory payroll authority when Long Tua is enabled.
- Auto-posting AI extraction without a human confirm.
- Derailing this product’s roadmap for a suite date.
